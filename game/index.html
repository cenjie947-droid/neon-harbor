Steal it. Drive it. Lose them.

Cole Vance is the best getaway driver in Neon Harbor, a sun-soaked coastal city of downtown towers, a harbor pier, and quiet residential streets. His fixer Mara Quinn has five jobs for him — and rival boss Dario Venn wants every one of them stopped. Take the jobs, take the cars, and do not get caught.
